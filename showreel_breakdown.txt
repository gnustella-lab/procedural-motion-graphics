MOTION DESIGN SHOWREEL 2026: BREAKDOWN
=======================================

Output:    showreel.mp4  (1920x1080, 30 fps, 15.0 s, 450 frames, H.264 / yuv420p)
Source:    showreel.py   (one Python file, about 400 lines, Pillow + NumPy + imageio-ffmpeg)

Every frame is generated in code. There's no stock footage, no image assets and no
After Effects. Each frame is a pure function of time t, so the whole reel is
deterministic and re-renders the same way every time (random seeds are fixed per
frame). Run "python showreel.py" to rebuild it.


1. CREATIVE DIRECTION
---------------------
Palette:  near-black navy (#0A0A12) base, with hot pink #FF2E88, cyan #00E5FF,
          yellow #FFD600, violet #7C4DFF and off-white. The base is dark so the
          neon colours glow. The palette is limited so the whole reel reads as one brand.
Type:     Segoe UI Bold, a clean geometric sans that handles extreme size changes well.
Structure: six scenes of roughly 2-3 s each, arranged as a rising-energy arc:
          ignition -> rhythm -> message -> depth -> climax -> brand resolve.
          The cuts scene is the peak. The logo is the exhale.


2. SCENE BY SCENE
-----------------
00.0-02.4  "IGNITION": intro
  - A single white dot pops in with ease-out-back (overshoot). It squashes away
    with an ease-in-expo anticipation beat.
  - Three staggered shockwave rings (pink/cyan/yellow) expand with ease-out-expo.
    Their stroke width and opacity fall off over the ring's lifetime.
  - 220-particle burst. Each particle has its own speed, size and drag factor,
    plus fake gravity (+200*q^2 on y). Each one draws a line streak from its
    previous position, a cheap stand-in for directional motion blur.
  - "MOTION" letters drop one at a time (70 ms stagger) with a heavy ease-out-back
    bounce. While they move, the letters flicker between palette colours. Offset
    pink/cyan copies sit behind them as a chromatic 3D extrude.
  - Exit: the letters get thrown upward with ease-in-expo (anticipation -> exit).
  - Camera: an impact shake at the burst (decays over 0.35 s) plus a slow
    push-in zoom.

02.4-05.2  "SHAPE & RHYTHM": procedural grid
  - 16x9 grid of 144 shapes. They enter in a radial stagger: delay is
    proportional to distance from the centre, so the grid "blooms" outward.
  - Each cell runs a travelling sine wave (scale + colour blend). That gives the
    ripple, and every cell's colour is interpolated between two palette pairs.
  - Shape morphing: cells cycle triangle -> square -> hexagon -> circle (polygon
    vertex count steps 3/4/6/40) with a phase offset by distance, so the morph
    sweeps across the grid in waves.
  - Mixed fill and outline cells add texture and hierarchy.
  - Title uses a TRACK MATTE: the text is rendered to a luminance layer and
    multiplied by an expanding rectangle mask, so the word wipes on from the
    centre out (the same idea as an AE track matte).
  - Exit: the grid collapses in a right-to-left column stagger.

05.2-08.4  KINETIC TYPOGRAPHY: "DESIGN / ANIMATE / TELL STORIES"
  - Three 1.05 s beats. Each beat starts with a full-screen skewed colour panel
    wiping in (ease-out-expo) and ends with it wiping out the other side
    (ease-in-expo). The fast-in / fast-out pacing is what makes it snappy.
  - Per-letter staggered rise with ease-out-back, then a per-letter staggered
    exit upward. Letter positions come from real glyph advance widths, so
    tracking stays correct while the letters animate.
  - Swiss / HUD details: a "01 / 03" counter, a live frame counter and a
    progress bar that runs across all three beats. These small elements give
    the big type something to play against.
  - Subtle sine-based "breathing" camera zoom.

08.4-11.2  "SYSTEMS · PHYSICS · DEPTH": 3D orbital system
  - Three rings of 60 particles each, built in real 3D space. Each ring has its
    own X-axis tilt and Y-axis spin (rotation matrices written out by hand).
    The points are projected with a perspective divide (fov / (fov + z)).
  - Particles are depth-sorted (painter's algorithm). Size and brightness
    scale with depth, which gives atmospheric perspective: far points fade
    into the background colour.
  - Three sine-wave ribbons, each with an amplitude envelope (a product of two
    sines) so they writhe instead of oscillating evenly.
  - A white core pops in with ease-out-elastic, surrounded by fading
    concentric halo rings.
  - Exit: an ease-in-expo camera punch-in that hands off energy into the
    next scene.

11.2-13.2  CLIMAX: rapid-fire rhythmic cuts
  - A hard cut every 4 frames (7.5 cuts per second). Colours rotate through
    the palette in complementary pairs.
  - Three alternating graphic modes: radial speed lines (manga style),
    scrolling diagonal hazard stripes, and spinning concentric squares.
  - Single-word strobe type: BOLD / FAST / LOUD / SHARP / CLEAN / PUNCH /
    FLOW / IMPACT. Each word scales up a little over its 4-frame life, with
    a hard drop shadow.
  - Continuous camera shake keeps the energy up.

13.2-15.0  BRAND RESOLVE: logo
  - IRIS transition: a dark circle grows out of the yellow and swallows the
    frame (ease-in-out-cubic). It's a change of pace from the cut-heavy section.
  - Logo build: a hexagon draws itself on segment by segment (stroke-reveal /
    "trim paths" effect, with partial segments interpolated). Then the inner
    triangle springs in with ease-out-elastic while rotating into place.
  - "MOTION DESIGN" letters rise and fade in on a 25 ms stagger with
    ease-out-expo.
  - The yellow underline grows out from the centre, then "SHOWREEL 2026"
    fades up.
  - The logo holds for a beat, then fades to black.


3. GLOBAL COMPOSITING PIPELINE (applied to every frame)
-------------------------------------------------------
  1. Animated background: a drifting radial gradient whose centre moves along a
     Lissajous path. The scene supplies the tint, so the backdrop colour
     shifts with each scene.
  2. Scene layer drawn on a transparent RGBA canvas, then alpha-composited.
  3. Virtual camera: zoom about the centre (resize + crop) and positional
     shake (offset).
  4. Bloom / glow: the frame is downsampled 4x, Gaussian-blurred, thresholded
     (only values above 90 contribute) and added back. Neon colours bleed
     light this way.
  5. Chromatic aberration: the red and blue channels are shifted horizontally
     in opposite directions. The shift is always slightly on (2 px) and spikes
     to 16 px for 0.2 s after each scene cut, which reads as a glitch hit on
     the transition.
  6. Flash frames: a 2-frame white flash on every scene boundary to punctuate it.
  7. Vignette: radial darkening that pulls the eye to the centre.
  8. Film grain: 6 pre-baked Gaussian noise plates cycled per frame. This
     gives an organic texture and also reduces banding in the gradients
     after H.264 compression.
  9. Fade in from black (0.15 s) and fade out (0.25 s).


4. MOTION PRINCIPLES SHOWCASED
------------------------------
  - Easing library written from scratch: cubic in/out, expo in/out,
    back (overshoot), elastic. Nothing moves linearly.
  - Anticipation & follow-through: the dot squash before the burst, letters
    flung out with ease-in after settling with ease-out-back.
  - Overlapping action / offset: stagger on letters, radial stagger on the
    grid, and staggered shockwave rings.
  - Arcs & physics: gravity on particles, drag variation, orbital motion.
  - Rhythm & pacing: a deliberate energy curve, slow-fast-slow, ending in a
    held logo.
  - Continuity: each exit leads into the next entry (camera punch-ins,
    flash frames, an iris) so the reel feels like one piece.
  - Hierarchy: big hero type paired with small HUD/meta details.
  - Masks / track mattes, stroke reveals (trim paths), shape morphing,
    3D projection and depth sorting.


5. HOW TO REBUILD / TWEAK
-------------------------
  pip install numpy pillow imageio imageio-ffmpeg
  python showreel.py showreel.mp4

  - Timing of each scene: the SCENES table near the bottom of showreel.py.
  - Colours: the palette constants at the top.
  - Resolution/fps: W, H, FPS.
